◆ calculateSampleErrorStats()

void Data::Robot::calculateSampleErrorStats ( )

calculates the sample mean and sample variance of the error for all the odometry and tracking measurements.

The sample mean and sample variance are calculate as

\[\begin{align} \bar{x} &= \frac{\sum_{i\in n} x_i}{n}\\ \sigma^2 &= \frac{\sum_{i\in n} (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n-1},\end{align}\]

where \(x_i\) denotes the \(i\)-th element in the sample of size \(n\). The sample variance formulation uses Bessel correction.

Note
The calculation on the sample mean relies on the population of the Robot::error vector and therefore, Robot::calculateMeasurementError needs to be called before this function.

◆ removeOutliers()

void Data::Robot::removeOutliers ( )
private

Uses the interquartile range to remove outliers from the measurements.

This is done since some measurement errors are due to incorrect data assocation (associaating the wrong barcode to a robot) and therefore give an incorrect indication of the noise present in the range and bearing sensor.
